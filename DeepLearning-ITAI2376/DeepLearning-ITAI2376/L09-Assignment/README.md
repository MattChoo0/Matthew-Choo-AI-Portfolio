L09 is divided into three parts.
The first part is to "Run a random agent in the CartPole environment and answer three short questions regarding state, action, and reward".
The second part is to "Train a Q-Learning agent for 500 episodes, analyze learning curves, and conduct an experiment comparing different exploration rates. This section requires answering six questions, including a hypothesis written before the experiment".
The third part is to "Read a comparison between CartPole and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), and answer four reflection questions relating these observations to your Final project agent design".
This lab does not use DQN because it implements Q-Learning rather than DQN.
There is the prioritization where time is better spent on the Midterm Blueprint and the Final project.
The complexity where a full DQN implementation requires math and debugging depth that exceeds the course scope.
Then there is the learning focus where we have to understand the RL loop and why Reinforcement Learning works is more valuable than manual coding.
Lastly, the industry relevance where the lab aims to bridge the gap to RLHF, which is the mechanism used to train models like GPT-4o, Claude, and Llama.
After ruuning the lab, there is the reflection that we have to describe a concrete reward signal, and reason through the trade-offs.
